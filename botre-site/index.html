FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply personally.

How do I start?

Use the design studio to build your brief, or message us on WhatsApp with your idea. Our team will guide you from there.

What happens after I send my brief?

Our team reviews your design and sends a quotation. Crafting starts only once you approve it.

Do you deliver to the UK?

Yes. We deliver across Sri Lanka and to the United Kingdom. For other destinations, ask us on WhatsApp.

Can I recreate a saree I have seen?

Yes. Share a photo or link in the reference fields, and our team will advise how to recreate the look.

How long does a saree take?

It depends on the design and the weave. We confirm your delivery date with your quotation.

Are my reference photos private?

In this version, photos stay on your device. Attach them in the WhatsApp chat after you send your brief.
